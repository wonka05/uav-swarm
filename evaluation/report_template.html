Table of results at the selected target

What each set-up adds

Coverage
Share of the forest's open ground that has been inside some drone's sensor footprint (radius 5 cells) at least once.
Finished
Coverage reached the target before the step limit: 500 steps for the policy alone and the override, 1,500 once drones can return and recharge.
Obstacle hits
Every move a tree blocked, summed over the whole mission. evaluate.py reports a different number: how many drones were blocked on the last step only.
Near misses
Steps on which two airborne drones were closer than one cell.
Drones lost
Drones whose battery ran out away from the base, or that no longer had enough charge to get home when the mission ended.
Safety corrections
Moves the safety layer changed because they would have hit a tree, cut a corner between two trees, left the map or come too close to another drone.
